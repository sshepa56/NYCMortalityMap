# Mortality Map

This map visualizes mortality rate per 1000, medicaid coverage by percentage, and age-adjusted death rates per 1000. You can explore overlaps among death rates, insurance enrollment, and program type. The dots in each community district are sized to show death-rate magnitude. Hovering over the dot will allow you to view the underlying statistics.
